RAINBOW SIX SIEGE · COLLEGIATE LAN

BATTLE OF THE MID-ATLANTIC

Lackawanna College Esports began in 2018 and strives to be a collegiate powerhouse. Each year we host a Rainbow Six Siege LAN, welcoming teams to the Scranton, PA campus for two days of play.

Register a Team View the Bracket

This year's tournament runs October 24–25, bringing teams from across the region to compete in a two-day format: a Day 1 round robin group stage, followed by a seeded single-elimination playoff bracket on Day 2.

Whether you're chasing the trophy or just here to watch the plays, follow along all weekend — matches stream live below.
